# Project Overview: Employee Data Analysis

# Introduction

In this project, we analyzed ABC Company's employee data, which consisted of 458 rows and 9 columns. The aim was to clean the dataset, derive meaningful insights, and create visualizations that could guide the company in understanding workforce distribution, salary trends, and team dynamics.

# Preprocessing Steps

Correcting Data in the Height Column: To ensure data quality, the "Height" column was replaced with random values between 150 and 180.

# Data Validation: 
After fixing the height values, the dataset was reviewed for inconsistencies or missing values to maintain reliability for subsequent analysis.

# Analysis Tasks

# 1. Distribution of Employees Across Teams

We looked at how employees are distributed across various teams and calculated the percentage split of employees in each team relative to the total workforce. This helps identify which teams are the largest and likely the most critical.

# 2. Employee Positions

Employees were grouped based on their roles within the company, allowing us to see the most common and specialized positions. This analysis sheds light on the organizational structure and skill distribution.

# 3. Predominant Age Group

Employees were categorized into age groups (18-25, 26-35, etc.) to find the most represented age bracket. This analysis reveals the company’s workforce demographics and hiring trends.

# 4. Salary Expenditure by Team and Position

The total salary expenses for each team and position were calculated to identify which ones accounted for the highest expenditures. This highlights areas where investments in personnel are most concentrated.

# 5. Correlation Between Age and Salary

We examined the relationship between employees' ages and their salaries to identify patterns and anomalies. Understanding this correlation helps gauge career progression and equity.

# Graphical Representations

Visuals were a key component of this analysis, making it easier to communicate the findings:

Team Distribution: A bar chart showing how many employees work in each team.

Position Segregation: A bar chart displaying the distribution of roles within the company.

Age Group Analysis: A bar chart highlighting the dominant age group among employees.

Salary Expenditure: Bar charts showing salary breakdowns by team and position.

Age vs. Salary Correlation: A scatterplot and heatmap visualizing how salary changes with age, revealing trends and outliers.

# Insights Gained

Team Distribution: Teams are not evenly staffed, with certain teams having significantly more employees. This suggests those teams are likely pivotal to the company’s operations.

Position Insights: The company’s workforce is concentrated in specific roles, such as managers and developers, reflecting its focus on specialized expertise.

Age Group Trends: Employees are predominantly aged 26-35, indicating a preference for mid-career professionals who balance experience with energy.

Salary Allocation: Key teams and roles account for the majority of salary expenditure, highlighting their importance to the business strategy.

Age-Salary Relationship: There is a positive correlation between age and salary, as expected with career growth. However, outliers in the data suggest unique cases, such as exceptional performers or salary inconsistencies.
